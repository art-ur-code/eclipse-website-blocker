Eclipse — Website Blocker doesn't just block distracting websites. It makes you pause, think, and be honest with yourself before visiting them.

🚫 Add any distracting website to your block list. When you try to open it, Eclipse stops you with a full-screen pause screen and asks you to:

• Explain why you need to visit the site right now
• Write a minimum number of characters to make sure your reason is intentional, not just an impulse
• Choose how long you'll allow yourself to stay (5–60 minutes)
• Confirm that you're making a conscious choice

A live character counter shows your progress and turns green once you've written enough. Pasting is disabled, so your reason has to be typed by you, not copied in.

Once you unlock the site, a slim reminder bar stays fixed at the bottom of the page, showing your reason and a live countdown. When time runs out, the site is automatically blocked again. You can also press STOP at any time to end your session early.

✨ Why Eclipse works

• Adds friction, not walls — access websites when you genuinely need them, while interrupting automatic, mindless visits
• Makes you articulate your intention — writing down a real reason forces you to stop and think, and pasting is blocked so you can't skip it
• Keeps you accountable — your reason stays visible while you're on the site
• Time-boxes your usage — set a limit so "just five minutes" doesn't quietly become an hour
• One-click blocking — open the popup on any website and block it instantly with the "Block this site" button
• Configurable minimum length — choose how many characters your unlock reason must contain (50 by default)
• Available in 11 languages — English, Spanish, Portuguese (Portugal and Brazil), French, German, Italian, Russian, Chinese (Simplified), Japanese and Hindi. Eclipse follows your browser's language, or you can choose one in Settings
• Simple, tidy popup — a home screen for quick blocking, a dedicated Blocked sites list, and a Settings screen
• Syncs your block list — blocked websites sync across your signed-in Chrome browsers
• Privacy-focused — no accounts, no tracking, no ads. Your data stays in Chrome's local and sync storage

🎯 Perfect for anyone who wants to spend less time on social media, news, YouTube, or other distracting websites while still allowing intentional access when it's genuinely needed.

How it works:

1. Open a distracting website, click the Eclipse icon and press "Block" — or add websites manually in Blocked sites
2. Try visiting a blocked website
3. Type your reason and meet the minimum character requirement
4. Choose your time limit and unlock the site
5. Stay accountable with the reminder bar and countdown
6. Stop early whenever you want, or let Eclipse block the site automatically when time runs out

Take a mindful pause before every distraction — with Eclipse.
